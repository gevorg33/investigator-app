> **Not legal advice. Not for publication.** Structured draft for counsel. Bracketed items
> are unresolved — see `counsel-brief.md`.

Governs the relationship between a user and the platform. Commercial terms between customers
and investigators are in the Terms & Conditions.

---

## 1. Acceptance

Using the platform requires acceptance of these terms, the Terms & Conditions, the Privacy
Policy and the Lawful Use Policy. Acceptance is required before registration completes.

Acceptance is recorded with the document version, the exact text shown, the language shown,
and the time. [**Counsel: minimum age and capacity requirements.**]

## 2. Accounts

- One account may hold both the customer and the investigator role.
- Account information must be accurate and kept current.
- Credentials must not be shared. A user is responsible for activity under their account.
- Suspected compromise must be reported promptly.
- Investigator capabilities require completed verification and acceptance of the
  investigator-specific terms at role activation.

## 3. What the platform provides

Tools to publish missions, receive and compare quotes, form assignments, communicate, handle
payment, and exchange evidence and reports securely — together with verification of
investigators and screening of missions.

The platform does not perform investigations, employ investigators, direct their methods, or
guarantee any outcome. Availability of investigators in any location or specialty is not
guaranteed.

## 4. Acceptable use

Users must not:

- use the platform for any unlawful purpose
- attempt to access data, accounts or systems they are not authorised to access
- interfere with, probe, overload or circumvent platform security or rate limits
- scrape, harvest or bulk-extract platform data
- misrepresent identity, licensing or qualifications
- circumvent the platform for payment or engagement
- upload malware, or content they are not entitled to share
- use the platform to harass any person, including staff
- use automated means to interact with the platform except as expressly permitted

## 5. The AI assistant

The assistant answers from platform documentation and from live platform data, within the
permissions of the user asking. It requires explicit confirmation before any action that
commits the user.

It does not provide legal advice, does not determine whether a request is lawful in a given
jurisdiction, and does not guarantee outcomes or prices. Where it has no source for an
answer, it says so rather than supplying one.

## 6. Content and materials

Users retain rights in the content they upload. Users grant the platform the rights
necessary to store, process, transmit and display that content in order to provide the
service, and to retain it as required by the retention policy or by law.

Users warrant that they are entitled to upload what they upload.

## 7. Availability

The platform is provided on an as-available basis. Maintenance, updates and interruptions
occur. [**Counsel: any uptime commitment, or explicit absence of one.**]

## 8. Suspension and termination

The platform may suspend or terminate access for breach of these terms, the Terms &
Conditions or the Lawful Use Policy. Grounds and process are set out in the Terms &
Conditions.

A user may close their account at any time. Closure does not discharge obligations under
live assignments, nor remove records the platform is required to retain.

## 9. Privacy

Processing of personal data is described in the Privacy Policy, which forms part of these
terms.

Users who receive personal data through the platform — including customers receiving
evidence and reports — have their own obligations to the people that data concerns.
