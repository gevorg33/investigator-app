## 2. What is processed

**Account** — name, email, password hash, phone where given, locale, roles, sessions and
device metadata, IP addresses.

**Investigator** — identity documents, licences and registrations, service areas,
specialties, services, languages, availability, pricing, ratings.

**Mission** — descriptions written by customers, locations, timeframes, budgets,
attachments. Mission descriptions routinely contain personal data about people who are not
platform users.

**Evidence** — photographs, video, documents and records produced by investigators. Contains
identifiable people, including incidental bystanders. Capture times and, where lawful,
capture locations.

**Reports** — findings concerning identifiable people.

**Communications** — messages between users and their attachments.

**Financial** — payment and payout records and ledger entries. No raw card data is stored;
card details go directly to the payment provider.

**Operational** — audit records of significant actions, including staff access to evidence,
and consent records.

## 3. People who are not users

The platform processes personal data about people who are the subject of an investigation
and who have no account and no relationship with the platform.

[**Counsel: this is the most significant issue in this document. Required: lawful basis and
a documented legitimate interests assessment; whether indirect-collection notice is owed and
which exemption applies; how subject access, objection and erasure requests from
investigation subjects are handled, including where evidence is subject to a live dispute or
a legal obligation to retain.**]

## 4. Why it is processed and on what basis

[**Counsel: to be completed per category. Engineering's understanding of purposes —
providing the service and performing the contract; verifying investigators; screening
missions for lawfulness; preventing fraud and abuse; processing payments; resolving
disputes; meeting legal obligations; securing the platform.**]

Marketing communications, where offered, rest on separate consent that is requested
independently of contract acceptance and is independently withdrawable.

## 5. Who it is shared with

- The other party to an assignment, to the extent the assignment requires
- The payment provider, for processing payments and payouts
- The media storage provider, which stores files as private resources with no public access
- Infrastructure and error-monitoring providers
- Staff, under recorded access grants for a stated purpose
- Regulators or law enforcement where legally required or appropriate

Evidence and reports are not shared beyond the assignment's participants except under a
recorded staff grant, or where legally compelled.

[**Counsel: full processor list, contractual basis for each, and disclosure obligations.**]

## 7. Retention

[**Blocking decision. A period is required for each of: identity and verification documents;
evidence; reports; mission descriptions; messages and attachments; financial and ledger
records; audit logs; consent records; data of deleted accounts.**]

Consent records are intended to survive account deletion as evidence of the basis on which
processing occurred.

## 8. Rights

[**Counsel: to be completed per applicable regime — access, rectification, erasure,
restriction, portability, objection, withdrawal of consent, complaint to a supervisory

users, since the conflicts differ.**]

## 9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Evidence, reports and verification documents are
stored as private resources with no public URL, and are served only through short-lived
links issued after a permission check.

Access is controlled per resource rather than by role alone. Staff access to evidence
requires an explicit recorded grant tied to a stated reason and is visible to the customer.
Sensitive actions are audited. Audit and consent records are append-only.

## 10. Automated decision-making

Missions are screened automatically for completeness and for indicators of prohibited
activity. Automated screening can hold a mission for human review; **rejection and policy
decisions are recorded with a reason and are subject to human review.**

Investigator discovery ranks results by declared criteria and distance. It does not make
decisions producing legal effects about users.

[**Counsel: whether any of this engages automated-decision-making provisions, and what
disclosure is required.**]

## 11. Changes

Material changes require acceptance before continued use, with notice before the effective
date. Each acceptance records the version, the exact text shown, and the language shown.
