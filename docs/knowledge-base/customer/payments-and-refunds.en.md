# Payments and refunds

## When am I charged?

After you accept a quote. Accepting a quote confirms the scope and price, and payment is
taken at that point. The assignment — the actual commitment between you and the
investigator — is created only once payment has been authorized successfully.

You are not charged for creating a mission, for receiving quotes, or for messaging an
investigator before you accept.

## What exactly am I paying for?

The price in the quote you accepted, plus any platform fees and taxes applicable to your
transaction. The full amount and its breakdown are shown before you confirm. You are never
charged an amount you have not been shown.

The price covers the scope stated in that quote. Work outside that scope requires a new
quote.

## Are there fees on top of the investigator's price?

The platform charges a fee on transactions. The exact amount is shown in your payment
breakdown before you confirm, together with any applicable taxes.

Fees are not deducted from what you were quoted — the investigator's price is what the
investigator is quoting for their work, and fees are shown separately.

## What payment methods can I use?

Payment is handled by our payment provider. The methods available depend on your country
and are shown at checkout.

The platform never sees or stores your full card details. Those go directly to the payment
provider.

## Why does my payment show as pending?

Because a payment is confirmed by our payment provider, not by your browser or app. There
can be a short delay between you completing the payment and us receiving that confirmation.

Your assignment is created once confirmation arrives. If a payment stays pending for an
unusually long time, contact support rather than paying again — paying twice creates a
refund you then have to wait for.

## Can I get a refund?

It depends on when you ask and what has happened. Each quote states its own cancellation
terms, and those terms govern your refund — which is why they are worth reading before you
accept.

Refunds can be full or partial. A partial refund reflects work already carried out. If you
cancel before the investigator has started, you are in a much stronger position than if you
cancel after evidence has been gathered.

## What if the investigator does not deliver?

Raise it first as a revision request, then as a dispute if that does not resolve it. See
the disputes article.

A dispute pauses the normal completion flow and brings staff in to review what was agreed
and what was delivered. Outcomes can include a revision, a partial refund, or a full
refund.

## Can I pay an investigator outside the platform?

No, and you should not. Payments made outside the platform have no record here, so they
are not covered by the dispute process, the evidence chain, or any refund route.

An investigator asking you to pay them directly is a reason to stop and contact support.

## What happens to my money if a mission is cancelled before work starts?

If you cancel before the investigator has accepted or started, you should expect a refund
under that quote's cancellation terms. If the investigator cancels, or fails to accept
within the agreed time, you are refunded.

Refunds return to the original payment method. The time it takes to appear depends on your
bank or card issuer, not on us.
