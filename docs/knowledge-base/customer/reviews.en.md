# Reviews

## How do I review an investigator?

From the assignment, once it is completed. You give a rating from 1 to 5 stars, and you can add
some words about the work if you want to. A rating on its own is fine.

You can review each assignment once. There is no review of a mission that never became an
assignment, and none while the work is still under way.

## When does my review appear on the investigator's profile?

The rating counts straight away: it is added to the investigator's average and review count as soon
as you submit it.

Your words wait for a moderator first. Until one publishes them, only you and the investigator can
read them. A moderator may hide words that name people, disclose details of the case, or break the
platform's rules; if that happens you are told why, and your rating still counts.

## Will the investigator or other customers know it was me?

The investigator knows, because the review belongs to your assignment with them. Other customers do
not: a review appears as written by "a customer of that assignment", with no name and no link to your
account or your other missions.

Do not put anything in your words that identifies you, the person you asked about, or the details of
the case. The rating and a sentence about how the work went are what help other customers.

## Can I change or delete my review later?

No. A review is kept as you wrote it, so that the investigator's response always answers what you
actually said. Think about it before you submit.

If you believe your review should come down — for example, you posted it on the wrong assignment —
contact support. Only staff can remove a review, and they record why.

## Can the investigator reply?

Yes, once. Their response is moderated in the same way and appears under your review once it is
published.

If their published response names you, discloses your case, or is abusive, you can report it from the
assignment. It leaves the profile until a moderator has looked at it again.

## Can a bad review get me my money back?

No. A review moves no money and reopens nothing. If the work was not what was agreed, ask for a
revision, then raise a dispute — see the article on revisions and disputes. You can do that and still
leave a review.

## Do ratings decide which investigators I am shown?

Not at the moment. A profile shows its average rating and how many reviews it has, but search results
are still ordered by distance, or by experience when you give no location. Ratings help you choose
between investigators you are shown; they do not decide who is shown.
