# Notifications and emails

## When will I be told that something happened?

When something happens to your mission or assignment that you did not do yourself:

- a moderator publishes your mission, returns it to you for changes, or rejects it;
- the investigator accepts or declines the assignment;
- the investigator submits their report.

Each one is recorded as a notification in your account and, unless you have turned these emails off,
sent to you by email. Things you do yourself — editing, cancelling, accepting a quote — are not
notified to you.

## What does a notification email contain?

Only what kind of thing happened, and a link to it in the app. It never contains the mission's
details, a message, a report, evidence, a reason someone wrote, or anyone's name. To see those you
open the link and sign in.

This is deliberate: email is read on lock screens, forwarded and stored by other companies. If an
email claiming to be from us contains details of your case, or asks for your password, it is not
from us.

## In which language are the emails?

In the language set on your account, whoever caused the notification. Emails are available in
English, Russian and Armenian; any other setting receives English.

## How do I stop these emails?

Every notification email ends with a link to stop these emails, and most email apps also show an
unsubscribe button for it. Opening the link only asks; the email stops when you press the
button on that page. It stops activity emails for that account in that workspace — nothing else.

You still get the emails your account needs to work, whatever you choose: confirming your email
address, resetting your password, and invitations. Those say at the bottom that they are account
emails.

A choice of which emails you get, and a notification list you can open in the app, are not in the
app yet. Until then, the unsubscribe link is how to stop activity emails.

## Why did I not receive an email?

- Your email address is not confirmed yet. Notification emails are only sent to a confirmed address.
- You unsubscribed from activity emails in this workspace.
- It was something you did yourself.
- It went to your spam folder.

If none of these applies, contact support.
