# Blocking an investigator

## How do I block an investigator?

Open their profile and choose **Block this investigator**. You are asked to confirm first.

You can see and undo your blocks in **Account → People you blocked**. Unblocking takes effect at
once.

## What happens when I block someone?

- They no longer appear when you search for investigators.
- They no longer see your published missions, and cannot send you new quotes.
- You cannot accept a quote they sent before you blocked them. If you want to, unblock them first.

## Will the investigator know I blocked them?

No. They are not told, and nothing they see says so. Your missions simply stop appearing to them.

## Does blocking end an assignment that is already under way?

No. Blocking is about what happens next. It never ends work that is already agreed, cancels a
payment, or releases anyone from what they owe — otherwise it could be used to walk away from an
assignment.

If you block an investigator while an assignment with them is still under way, the assignment
continues and our team reviews it. They decide what happens: the work continues, it is passed to
another investigator, or it is cancelled with any refund decided on its merits.

## Is blocking the same as reporting?

No. Blocking means "not this person" and only changes what you see and what they can do towards
you. It does not tell our team anything is wrong. Reporting someone to our team from the app is not
available yet; until it is, contact support if someone has broken the platform's rules.
