# How quotes work

## What is a quote?

A quote is a formal offer from an investigator for your mission. It states the price, the
estimated duration, the proposed scope, what will be delivered, any assumptions and
exclusions, an expiry date, and the cancellation terms. You can receive several quotes for
one mission and compare them before choosing.

## How long does a quote stay valid?

Each quote carries its own expiry date, set by the investigator when they submit it. After
that date the quote can no longer be accepted, and attempting to accept it will fail. If a
quote you wanted has expired, you can ask the investigator to submit a new one — the
original cannot be reinstated.

## Can I accept more than one quote for the same mission?

No. Accepting a quote creates a single assignment for that mission. Once an assignment
exists, the other quotes for that mission are closed. If you want work from more than one
investigator, create a separate mission for each piece of work.

## What happens when I accept a quote?

Accepting a quote confirms the scope and price. An assignment is created only after your
acceptance and a successful payment authorization. Until both have happened, no
investigator is committed to the work and no assignment exists.

## Why can't I see a price before requesting quotes?

Prices are set by each investigator for your specific mission, based on its scope,
location and duration. There is no fixed price list, and no price is quoted until an
investigator reviews your mission. The assistant cannot estimate a price for you — any
figure before a quote would be a guess.

## Can I negotiate a quote?

You can message the investigator within the mission to discuss scope before accepting. If
you agree on different terms, the investigator submits a revised quote. Changes agreed in
messages are not binding until they appear in a quote you accept.
