# Troubleshooting

## I am not receiving the verification email

Check your spam folder first, and confirm the address you registered is the one you are
checking. Request a new verification email from the sign-in screen — the previous link may
have expired.

If nothing arrives after several attempts, the address may be blocked by your provider.
Contact support from a different address and tell them which address you registered.

## I cannot sign in

Confirm you are using the right email address, then reset your password. If a reset email
does not arrive, the account may be registered to a different address than you remember.

Repeated failed attempts temporarily lock sign-in to protect the account. Wait and try
again rather than continuing — further attempts extend the lock.

## My mission is not receiving any quotes

Usually one of: the area has few investigators covering it, the budget range is below what
the work costs there, the timeframe is too tight, or the description is too vague for
anyone to price.

Widening the area, extending the timeframe, or adding detail about what you need are the
things most likely to help. The assistant can tell you whether investigators matching your
requirements exist at all, which distinguishes "nobody available" from "nobody interested".

## My mission was rejected and I do not understand why

The rejection states its reason. The most common one is that the request falls into a
prohibited category — often because of how it was described rather than what was intended.

Read the lawful use policy, then revise your mission to state your lawful purpose and your
relationship to the matter. If you still think the rejection is wrong, contact support with
the mission reference.

## I cannot open my evidence or report

Access links are short-lived and are issued for you specifically. A link you saved,
bookmarked, or copied from an earlier session will stop working — open the item from inside
the assignment instead.

If opening it from the assignment also fails, check the assignment is one you still have
access to. If it is, contact support with the assignment reference.

## A file will not upload

Check the file type and size are within the limits shown on the upload screen, and that
your connection is stable. Large video files are the most common cause.

Uploads are scanned before they become available, so a file that appears to have uploaded
may be briefly unavailable. If it stays unavailable for a long time, or is rejected, the
scan may have flagged it.

## I paid but my assignment has not appeared

Payment is confirmed by our payment provider rather than by your app, so there can be a
short delay.

Do not pay again. If it has not resolved after a reasonable wait, contact support with the
date, amount and mission reference — paying twice creates a refund you then wait for.

## I was charged an amount I did not expect

Check the payment breakdown on the assignment, which shows the investigator's price, the
platform fee and any tax separately.

If the total does not match what you were shown before confirming, contact support with the
assignment reference. You should never be charged an amount you were not shown.

## My investigator has stopped responding

Message them through the assignment so there is a record, then contact support if there is
still no response after a reasonable period.

Do not chase them outside the platform. Staff can see the assignment history and can
intervene, including reassigning or refunding where work has been abandoned.

## I am not receiving notifications

Check your notification preferences in settings, and your device's permission for the app.
Email notifications may also be landing in spam.

Notifications tell you something happened without revealing its content, so even with them
working you will need to open the app to read a message.

## Something looks wrong with my account

If you suspect someone else has access: change your password, revoke unrecognised sessions
in settings, and contact support immediately.

For anything else — a mission you do not recognise, a charge you did not make — contact
support with the reference and do not take further action on it in the meantime.
