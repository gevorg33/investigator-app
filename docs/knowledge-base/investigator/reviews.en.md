# Reviews of your work

## When can a customer review me?

Once an assignment is completed, its customer can rate it from 1 to 5 and add words. Each assignment
can be reviewed once. Nobody can review you for a mission you only quoted on, or while the work is
still in progress.

## What do other customers see on my profile?

Your average rating and how many reviews you have, and each review's rating with its words and your
response once a moderator has published them. Reviews are shown newest first. The reviewer is shown
only as "a customer of that assignment".

Your profile has to be published for any of this to show. While it is a draft, your reviews are
visible only to you and the customers who wrote them.

## Why can't I see the customer's words on my profile yet?

Every review's words — and your response — wait for a moderator before they are public. You can
read them on the assignment straight away. The rating counts in your average from the moment it is
submitted.

## Can I respond to a review?

Yes, once, from the assignment. Keep it factual and about the work: what was agreed, what was
delivered, what limited what could be established. Your response is moderated too, and it cannot be
edited afterwards, so write it for a customer reading it months from now.

Do not name the customer, the subject of the investigation, or any detail of the case. A response
that does will be hidden, and you will be told why.

## What can I do about a review I think is unfair?

If the published words name you personally, disclose the case, are abusive, or are plainly about a
different investigator, report them from the assignment and say why. The words leave your profile
until a moderator has looked again. The rating stays while the moderator decides.

Disagreeing with a low rating is not a reason to report it. Respond instead — a calm, specific
response usually does more for your profile than the review does against it.

If a review should not be there at all, for example because it was posted by mistake, contact
support. Only staff can remove a review, and they record why.

## Can a customer change their review after I respond?

No. A review is never rewritten, which is what keeps your response answering what was actually said.

## Do ratings affect who finds me?

Not yet. Customers see your rating when they look at your profile, but search results are ordered by
distance, or by experience when no location is given. What decides whether you appear at all is
still your verified specialties, service areas, languages and availability.
