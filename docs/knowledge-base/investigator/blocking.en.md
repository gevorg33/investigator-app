# Blocking a customer

## How do I block a customer?

In **Missions**, open **More actions** on one of their missions and choose **Block this customer**.
You are asked to confirm first. The customer is not named: you block "the customer of this
mission".

You can see and undo your blocks in **Account → People you blocked**, where a customer you blocked
from a mission is listed as "A customer". Unblocking takes effect at once.

## What happens when I block a customer?

- Their published missions no longer appear to you, and you cannot quote on them.
- Any quotes you had sent them and that are still open are withdrawn. To the customer this looks
  the same as withdrawing a quote yourself.
- You no longer appear when they search for investigators.

## Will the customer know I blocked them?

No. They are not told, and nothing they see says so.

## Does blocking end an assignment that is already under way?

No. Blocking never ends agreed work or releases you from it, and it does not change the payment.
You are still expected to carry out an assignment you accepted.

If you block a customer while an assignment with them is under way, it continues and our team
reviews it. They decide whether the work continues, is passed to another investigator, or is
cancelled — and on what terms.

## If I work for an agency, does my block apply to the agency?

No. A block is yours, as a person. It applies wherever you work — in your own workspace and in any
agency — but other members of your agency are not affected, and cannot see your blocks.

## Is blocking the same as reporting?

No. Blocking only changes what you and that customer see of each other. It does not tell our team
anything is wrong. Reporting someone from the app is not available yet; until it is, contact
support.
