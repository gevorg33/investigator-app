# Troubleshooting

## My verification has not been reviewed yet

Review is done by staff, not automatically, so it is not immediate. Check that every
required document is uploaded, legible, unexpired, and covers the areas and specialties you
declared.

Mismatches between declarations and documents are the usual cause of a stalled review. If
everything matches and it has been an unusually long time, contact support with your
verification reference.

## I am verified but not appearing in customer results

Check in this order: your service areas actually cover the places customers are searching,
your availability is current, your specialties and services match how customers describe
their needs, and no required document has since lapsed.

Profile text does not affect whether you appear — only the hard requirements do. Most cases
are stale availability or a service area drawn tighter than you actually work.

## I am getting missions that are not relevant to me

Your declarations are too broad somewhere. Narrow the service areas to where you genuinely
work, and remove specialties or services you do not actually want.

Declining is expected occasionally, but a high decline rate affects how missions are routed
to you. Fixing the profile is better than declining repeatedly.

Refusals on substantiated policy grounds — unlawful or unauthorised customer material — are
excluded from that count.

## I cannot submit a quote

Confirm your verification is current, the mission has not expired or been withdrawn, and you
still meet its requirements — a lapsed document removes eligibility immediately.

If the mission is still open and you believe you qualify, contact support with the mission
reference.

## My evidence upload failed

Check the file type and size against the limits shown, and your connection. Large video is
the most common failure.

Uploads are scanned before they become available, so a file may appear uploaded but not yet
open. If it is rejected, re-encoding from the original source usually clears a false
positive. If it keeps failing, contact support rather than trying repeatedly.

## The customer is not responding to my submitted report

Prompt them through the assignment so there is a record. Assignments do not pay out while
they sit in customer review.

If there is still no response after a reasonable period, contact support with the assignment
reference — staff can complete an assignment a customer has abandoned.

## My payout has not arrived

Check the assignment has actually completed, that your payout details are complete and
valid, and the payout status in your earnings view.

Bank transfer timing is outside the platform's control. If the status says paid and nothing
has arrived after a reasonable period, contact support with the payout reference.

## My earnings do not match what I expected

Open the breakdown for the specific assignment. It shows your price, the platform fee and
any tax separately.

If the breakdown itself looks wrong, raise it with the assignment reference. If the
difference is a refund, the dispute or cancellation record will show why.

## I need to change my service area or specialties

Both are editable in your profile. Removing takes effect immediately; adding something that
needs additional proof goes through review, and your existing verified scope is unaffected
meanwhile.

## A customer asked me to work off-platform

Decline and report it. Off-platform work has no dispute route, no chargeback defence and no
record that anything was agreed.

It is also grounds for suspension — for you, not only for them.
