# Notifications and emails

## When will I be told that something happened?

When a customer's payment makes you a new assignment. It is recorded as a notification in your
account and, unless you have turned these emails off, sent to you by email, with a link to your
missions.

Notifications for other events — a quote accepted, a new message, a verification about to expire —
are not sent yet.

## What does a notification email contain?

Only what kind of thing happened, and a link to it in the app. It never contains the mission's
details, the customer's name, a message or evidence. You sign in to see those. If an email claiming
to be from us contains case details, or asks for your password, it is not from us.

## If I work for an agency, who else sees my notifications?

Nobody. A notification is yours alone, in the workspace it happened in — not your agency's owner,
and not other members. If you leave the agency, you stop receiving its notifications.

## In which language are the emails?

In the language set on your account. Emails are available in English, Russian and Armenian; any
other setting receives English.

## How do I stop these emails?

Every notification email ends with a link to stop these emails. Opening it only asks; the email
stops when you press the button on that page. It stops activity emails in that workspace only.

Account emails — confirming your address, resetting your password, invitations to an agency — are
always sent.

## Why did I not receive an email?

- Your email address is not confirmed yet.
- You unsubscribed from activity emails in this workspace.
- You are no longer an active member of the workspace the assignment belongs to.
- It went to your spam folder.
