# Your responsibilities

> Plain-language summary. The Terms of Service, Terms & Conditions and Lawful Use Policy are
> the authority, and govern if this summary differs from them.

## What is the platform's role?

It connects customers with independent investigators and provides the tools they use to
agree scope, transact, communicate and exchange deliverables. It verifies investigators and
screens missions before they are published.

It does not perform investigations, employ investigators, or direct how work is carried out.
Investigators are independent professionals responsible for their own methods and their own
legal compliance.

## What am I responsible for as a customer?

Requesting only work that is lawful where it will be carried out, and describing it
accurately — including anything that affects whether it is lawful.

You are also responsible for what you do with what you receive. Obtaining information
lawfully does not make every later use of it lawful, and evidence about identifiable people
carries data-protection obligations that pass to you.

## What am I responsible for as an investigator?

Holding every licence and permission required where you work, working within that scope,
and complying with the law on surveillance, recording, data protection and property.

You must decline and report work that would require unlawful activity. Neither a customer's
instruction nor the fact that a mission was published is authorisation to act unlawfully.

## Does the platform check that my request is legal?

Missions are screened before publication, and some are held for review by staff or rejected.
That screening is designed to catch prohibited requests — it is not legal advice and it is
not a determination that your request is lawful.

You remain responsible for the lawfulness of what you request. The platform does not, and
cannot, assess your specific circumstances against your jurisdiction's law.

## Does verification mean the platform vouches for an investigator?

No. Verification confirms that documents establishing an investigator's identity and
professional standing were checked at the time of review.

It is not a guarantee of competence, quality or outcome, and it does not move an
investigator's professional or legal responsibility onto the platform.

## What happens if I break these rules?

Accounts can be suspended or terminated for requesting, offering or performing prohibited
activity, for false licensing information, for taking payment off-platform, for mishandling
evidence, or for abusive conduct.

Decisions are recorded with a reason and communicated to you. Where the law requires it, or
where the matter is serious, regulators or law enforcement may be informed.

## How do I report something?

Every mission, message, profile and assignment has a reporting control. Reports go to staff
with the relevant history attached.

If you are asked to do something unlawful, report it rather than simply declining — a single
refusal tells us nothing, whereas a pattern across users does.

## Do I have to accept the terms?

Yes. You cannot complete registration without accepting the Terms of Service, Terms &
Conditions, Privacy Policy and Lawful Use Policy.

If you later take on the investigator role, you accept the investigator-specific terms at
that point, because they did not apply to you before.

## What happens when the terms change?

You are notified. Where a change materially affects your rights, your liability, how your
data is used, or fees, you are asked to accept the new version before continuing to use the
platform.

We record which version you accepted, in which language, and when.
