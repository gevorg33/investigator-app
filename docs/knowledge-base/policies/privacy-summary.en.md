# Privacy summary

> Plain-language summary. The Privacy Policy is the authority and governs if this summary
> differs from it.

## What data does the platform hold about me?

Your account details, your missions and their history, quotes, assignments, payments,
messages, evidence and reports, and an audit record of significant actions on your account.

Investigators additionally have their verification documents, service areas, specialties,
languages and availability held.

## Who can see my evidence and reports?

You and the other party to that assignment. Nobody else by default, including staff.

Staff access requires a specific recorded reason — usually a dispute — and you can see in
your own access log that it happened. There is no mode in which staff browse evidence.

## How is my data protected?

It is encrypted in transit and at rest. Evidence, reports and verification documents are
stored privately with no public link, and are only reachable through short-lived links
issued after a permission check.

Access is controlled per item rather than by job title, and sensitive actions are recorded in
a log that cannot be edited.

## What about people who are the subject of an investigation?

The platform processes data about people who do not have accounts. Those people have rights
over that data in most jurisdictions.

Both the customer and the investigator have obligations to them. If you receive evidence
about someone, those obligations apply to you as well.

## Can I get a copy of my data, or delete my account?

Yes to both. Export is prepared as a background job and you are notified when it is ready.
Deletion removes your account and personal data, subject to what must legally be retained.

Some records survive deletion because the law requires it — financial records are the usual
example, and evidence inside a completed assignment may be retained under its own rule
because the other party is also involved.

## How long is my data kept?

Each type of data has its own retention period. Data is not kept indefinitely by default.

If you need something beyond its retention period, download it before then.

## Is my data used to train AI?

No. The assistant answers from platform documentation and from live data you are permitted
to see. Your messages, evidence and reports are not used as training material.

## Where is my data stored and processed?

Details are in the Privacy Policy, including any transfers outside your country and the
safeguards applied.

## Who do I contact about privacy?

The contact route is in the Privacy Policy. You can also raise a concern with the data
protection authority in your country.
