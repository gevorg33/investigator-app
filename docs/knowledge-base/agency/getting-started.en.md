# Setting up an agency

## What is an agency here?

A workspace your company works in, separate from your personal one. Missions, quotes,
assignments, reports and evidence that belong to the agency stay inside it, and the people
you bring in see only what their role allows.

Your personal workspace does not go anywhere. You can switch between it and any agency you
belong to, and the platform always shows which one you are working in.

## What do I need to create one?

Five things, and you can supply them as you go:

- the agency's name
- the country it operates from
- a business email address — where the platform writes to the company, not to you personally
- a time zone
- a currency

The agency exists from the moment you create it, and becomes usable once all five are
there. Until then it is still being set up, and the platform tells you what is missing.

Everything else — legal name, company type, size, languages, the areas and services you
cover, specialities, credentials, experience, working hours and branding — can be added
whenever you like.

## Am I the owner?

Yes. Whoever creates the agency is its owner, and an agency always has at least one active
owner: the platform will not let the last one be removed or suspended.

## What am I agreeing to?

The agency terms, which you accept as part of creating the agency. The platform records
which exact version and language you were shown, and you can read it again at any time.
Accepting the terms for an agency is separate from the terms you accepted for your own
account.

## Do I need to be an investigator?

No. An agency owner runs the company; whether you also work cases is up to you.

## Who else can join?

People you invite. Nobody can add themselves to your agency, and joining is always your
decision — invitations, roles and teams are covered in their own articles as they arrive.

## What can I not do yet?

Some of this is still being built. Today you can create an agency, complete the minimum,
and see it in your workspace list. Inviting employees, teams, agency-owned investigator
profiles and agency verification are arriving in later releases.
